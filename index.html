Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are quick answers to the most common queries from prospective families.

What board curricula are offered at Aurelia Academy?

We offer integrated IB (International Baccalaureate) Primary & Diploma programs, Cambridge IGCSE for Middle/Senior secondary, and Advanced Placement (AP) preparation for North American universities.

What is the teacher-to-student ratio?

To ensure individualized attention, we maintain a strict 1:12 teacher-to-student ratio in Primary grades and 1:15 in Middle & Senior wings.

Are school transport facilities available with tracking?

Yes, our fleet of air-conditioned buses is equipped with real-time GPS tracking, speed governors, female security attendants, and live mobile app notifications for parents.

Does the school offer merit scholarships or financial aid?

Aurelia Academy offers merit-based scholarships up to 50% tuition waiver for exceptional achievements in academics, STEM innovation, national sports, and fine arts.

What safety measures are implemented on campus?

The campus features 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric entry barriers, a full-time resident medical doctor with an emergency clinic, and certified safety officers.
